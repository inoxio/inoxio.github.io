## Services

We firmly believe that quality cannot be "applied" afterwards to a finished
product. Software quality can only be ensured if sufficient attention is paid
to it in every phase of the entire development process.

That is why you will find support with us in nearly all areas of software
development.

### Agile development

In software development it is still common to plan for a long time, document
requirements excessively and deliver something much later that hopefully matches
the original plan.

Not with us.

We develop and deliver software in short iterations. Stakeholders can give
feedback early, spot problems and initiate corrections. In cross-functional
teams all roles work hand in hand, so that the quality of the product can be
ensured from the very beginning.

_We build what the customer really needs. In many cases that is something
different from what they originally wanted._

### Agile testing

With a well-stocked toolbox, our colleagues support you by:

- critically questioning design and planning phases
- defining acceptance criteria
- coaching on writing efficient developer tests
- exploratory testing
- uncovering security vulnerabilities
- automating deployment processes

Last but not least, we know how to balance a test pyramid and where each aspect
can be tested most efficiently.
