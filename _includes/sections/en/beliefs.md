## Beliefs

<div class="cards" markdown="1">

<div class="card" markdown="1">
<span class="num">01</span>

### Agility

We firmly believe that agile methods help deliver software projects faster, more
predictably, more cheaply and in higher quality.

For us, agility does not mean _Scrum by the book_. We see agility as a
collection of practices and methods tailored to a team and its needs, evolving
together with that team over time.
</div>

<div class="card" markdown="1">
<span class="num">02</span>

### Cross-functional

We like working in cross-functional teams because all roles required for the
task at hand come together there. Tasks can be carried out quickly and
efficiently without external dependencies delaying progress.

We see ourselves as generalists (T-shaped people), which lets us spread many
different roles across a few team members. As a result, teams stay small, fast,
agile and powerful.
</div>

<div class="card" markdown="1">
<span class="num">03</span>

### You build it, you run it

We accompany development from design to operations, because that is the only
way we can guarantee delivering value to the customer in high quality.

Together with the team we take collective responsibility for the features we
build, especially during operations. Creating transparency through suitable
solutions such as monitoring and alerting helps us to fulfil this task.
</div>

<div class="card" markdown="1">
<span class="num">04</span>

### Continuous improvement

We promote continuous improvement within a team. Only if we want to improve
every single day can we achieve great things together. Retrospectives with root
cause analysis and without blame are therefore our tool of choice.

Small experiments with measurable results help to adapt a team's structure and
way of working to its needs.
</div>
