#### My profile:

- **Development and migration of (microservice) platforms**: Whether on-premises or cloud-based; classic or in Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure or Google Cloud Platform. Manual work is yesterday's news! _Infrastructure is nothing but code_ and that's exactly how it should be treated. I enjoy seeing things grow and improve. Terraform and CloudFormation are my tools of choice here.

- **Development and design of Continuous Integration, Delivery and Deployment pipelines**: Nothing is worse than developing something for ages without getting real feedback from customers. Pipelines help to provide services automatically and with optimal, consistent quality. They give fast feedback and are essential for finding out whether we're heading in the right direction or not.

- **Experienced testing expert**: In my own work I want to lead by example and write clean, readable and simple source code that is easy to maintain in the end. The right tests at the right time help me and our team instead of slowing us down or getting in our way. In pairing and in general exchange, I try to spread my "tester's eye" and the questioning mindset a tester needs. Beyond that, fast and constantly running tests in our Continuous Delivery pipelines are indispensable for increasing quality and minimizing risk.

- **Developer with a focus on quality**: I would never call myself a full-blooded developer, but I still like to switch to a developer's perspective. With Ruby or JavaScript I have already built various services. For other scripting languages out there, too, I can draw on a solid base of knowledge and experience, which I'm happy to share. My "tester's eye" is always present and watches over what's going on.

- **Agile ninja**: I am a Certified Scrum Master of the Scrum Alliance. From time to time, and especially with small and young teams, I can take on the role of Scrum Master. Developing shared ways of working and organizing and facilitating workshops, reviews or meetings contributes to team success. What matters most to me is that I stay close to the team and remain actively involved in the product myself.
