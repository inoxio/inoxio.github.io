#### Hi 👋,

my name is Meik Minks. For 12 years I have been working as a Delivery Engineer, Agile Tester,
Continuous \* (_everything_) expert and Scrum Master on various projects all over Germany.
Fortunately, I got to know and appreciate the agile way of working, and agile software development in a team,
early on. I'm convinced that it brings out the best in a cross-functional,
diverse group of people and can ultimately lead to a better product.

Both out of conviction and from my practical professional experience, I can say that
I like being an all-rounder in software development. For me, there are no separate roles for testers,
developers or administrators. The optimum is reached when these three roles are taken on fluidly by one or, even better, two people at the same time
while working on an issue.
