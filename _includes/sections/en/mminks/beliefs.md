#### What matters to me?

All my work follows these principles:

- **People**: Everything stands and falls with ourselves. With humanity, dignity, respect, empathy and openness, I build trust and lay the foundation for clear communication on equal footing. This creates a strong bond within our team, enables peak performance and ultimately leads to a better-quality product.

- **Being open minded**: No project is like another, so there can be no universal solutions. Every team member is valuable and contributes their part. I always look forward to working with smart and capable people from whom I can learn something myself. I equally enjoy regularly attending conferences and meetups or organizing dojos and mini workshops within our team.

- **Everything is Code**: Thankfully, the times when things were just quickly clicked together are over. I live by the principle "Everything is Code". Source code is transparent, versioned, testable and reproducible. That way, every area of software development (including infrastructure) can benefit from it.

- **Making mistakes is allowed (and encouraged)**: One of the most important points is being allowed to make mistakes. They are essential for learning and moving forward. So please, please: let's make mistakes! What matters is learning from them – as early and as quickly as possible. That's how we become experts in our field.

- **Having fun**: Fun and team spirit are incredibly important. A relaxed
  working environment and style contribute to that just as much as the after-work drink. All of this motivates me and our team to perform at our best and, in turn, leads to a better product.
